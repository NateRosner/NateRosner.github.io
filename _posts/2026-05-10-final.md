Project Name: Nate's Weather T-Shirt. 

This adhesive attachment can be placed on any t-shirt or tent to find the correct clothing to wear when the Weather App is not accessible. This piece uses Lilypad components. The temperature sensor reads the temperature when on, and the buzzer and RGB LED play different tones and light different colors, respectively, to indicate the temperature range and what clothes to wear. To save battery, a switch is included to allow the user to turn the device on and off. My motivation for this project is that I wanted something I could use in nature to get information without my phone. I think getting off electronics is a good idea, and this is a great way to do it. 

Supplies:

1 LilyPad Arduino
1 LilyPad Temp. Sensor
1 LilyPad Buzzer
1 LilyPad RGB LED
Conductive Thread 
Thread, Scissors, Needle
8x11 craft felt, white
Old T-shirt
Gorilla Glue Tape
